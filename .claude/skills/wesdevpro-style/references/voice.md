# Copy voice

First person, understated in delivery, confident in substance. Leads with the work. The point of view is **integration** — making parts work together so cleanly the seams disappear.

## Core belief (drives every angle)

> Anyone can build a part; the value is making the parts work together so cleanly you stop noticing the seams.

Talk about wiring systems together, removing seams, making things work as one — not just "building features."

## Rules

- **First person:** "I am Wesley Ford", "I currently work in fintech", "What I Focus On". Direct address for CTAs: "Let's Connect", "Reach out".
- **No hype:** no exclamation marks, no superlatives, no growth-marketing language, no emoji. Calm delivery, sharp substance.
- **80/20 subject split:** ~80% custom integrations + web design across the stack; ~20% open-source craft (Buefy / Vue 3 maintainership, the tinkerer streak) shown as proof of range, not noise.
- **Casing:** Title Case for page titles, section headings, nav items, button labels. Sentence case for body and subtitles. Eyebrows are UPPERCASE mono with the teal `/`.
- **Sentence shape:** one idea per sentence. Subtitles run 1–2 lines, ~58 characters wide.
- **Caption voice:** a caption states what the image shows and why it matters. It is not the nearby heading restated and it is not alt text. Present tense, sentence case, one or two sentences, capped at 44 characters of measure (`--measure-caption`). Never decorative.
- **Standfirst voice:** 30–50 words. Answers "why should I keep reading" in the buyer's terms. Not a summary of the piece and not a restatement of the headline.
- **Length guidance:** the density minimums in `SKILL.md` (60 words for an offer/service card, 40 for a process step, a 25-word standfirst for an index entry, 600 words for a case study) are the floor, not a suggestion. Terseness is still the register — sentences stay short and direct — the sections are just longer than the old minimal-layout brevity assumed. Below the minimum, the section isn't ready; mark it `TODO` rather than padding it out or shrinking the type to hide the gap.
- **CTA arrow:** ASCII `->`, not `→` and not an icon. "View Project ->", "Read Post ->".
- **Vocabulary:** integrate, wire together, seamless, dependable, clear, maintainable, full-stack, architecture, across the stack, end-to-end, long-term.
- **Contact framing:** email `wes@wesdevpro.com`, framed as "Let's Connect" — open to collaboration, consulting, and engineering roles.

## Examples

- Hero (legacy, still on the live site): "Building clear, dependable, user-focused software."
- Hero (current angle): "Custom integrations and web design — built so the parts work together and the seams stop showing."
- About: "Engineering work built on clarity, reliability, and maintainability."
- Contact: "Reach out for collaboration, consulting discussions, or software opportunities."
- Eyebrows: `/ SELECTED WORK` · `/ WRITING` · `/ ABOUT` · `/ CONTACT`

## Off-brand — rewrite on sight

- "🚀 Let's build something amazing!" → "Let's connect."
- "Blazing-fast, world-class solutions" → "Dependable systems, wired together cleanly."
- "We help teams..." (agency plural) → first person, singular.
- "Learn more" (vague) → "View Project ->", "Read Post ->".

## Banned register — luxury vocabulary

Editorial sits adjacent to luxury/lifestyle copy, and drift toward it is the obvious failure mode for this pass. Out, on top of the existing banned list:

elegant, refined, curated, bespoke, crafted, timeless, effortless, seamless experience, thoughtfully designed
