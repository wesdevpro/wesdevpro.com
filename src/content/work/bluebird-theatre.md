## What Was Built

A concept site for a local community theater, built on my own initiative
rather than as paid client work. It covers what a theater's site typically
needs: a productions listing, audition and volunteer information, venue and
showtime details, and information on the theater's youth program.

The site is live at bluebirdtheater.netlify.app as a concept, not a
delivered engagement.
